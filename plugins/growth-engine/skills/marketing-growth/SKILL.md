# Marketing & Growth

Frameworks that produce measurable results. All recommendations have benchmarks attached.

## Copywriting Frameworks: AIDA vs PAS

Choose based on **audience temperature**, not habit.

**AIDA** (Attention → Interest → Desire → Action):
- Best for: cold/top-of-funnel audiences who don't yet know they have the problem
- Use in: SEO content, cold ads, awareness campaigns, homepage hero
- Structure: Lead with something surprising or contrarian → build curiosity → show the transformation → one CTA
- AIDA wins 60–65% of A/B tests against PAS in cold contexts (Anyword 2025 analysis)

**PAS** (Problem → Agitate → Solution):
- Best for: warm, problem-aware audiences who know they're hurting
- Use in: product pages, case studies, free-trial conversion pages, re-engagement emails
- Structure: Name the exact pain → make it visceral (cost, risk, frustration) → reveal the relief
- PAS wins 35–40% of tests; outperforms AIDA in problem-urgency contexts

**Hybrid** (best practice for mid-funnel):
- Open with the problem (PAS hook) → build with AIDA structure
- Works when audience is aware of the problem category but not yet comparing solutions

**Copy fundamentals** (apply to both frameworks):
- Features → benefits translation: "2GB storage" → "never worry about hitting limits mid-project"
- Specificity beats vagueness: "saves 3 hours per week" > "saves time"
- Social proof adjacent to the CTA: testimonial + star rating within 100px of the button
- One CTA per page / per email — two CTAs split attention and halve conversion

## SEO: Topic Clusters Over Siloed Posts

The old model (one post per keyword) causes keyword cannibalization and dilutes topical authority. The cluster model compounds both.

**Topic cluster structure**:
```
Pillar page (broad topic, 2,500+ words):
  "Complete Guide to B2B Sales Automation"
    ↕ internal links ↕
  Cluster page: "ICP Definition: 5-Layer Framework"
  Cluster page: "Cold Email Benchmarks 2026"
  Cluster page: "Signal Stacking for Outbound Sales"
  Cluster page: "CRM Data Hygiene Checklist"
  Cluster page: "Sales Cadence: 8-Touch Multi-Channel Template"
```

**Why it works**: Google uses topical authority as a ranking signal. A pillar + 5–8 deeply linked clusters signals comprehensive coverage of a topic → higher rankings for all pages in the cluster.

**Benchmark**: Cluster approach delivers 25–40% organic traffic lift vs siloed strategy over 6–12 months.

**Implementation checklist**:
- [ ] Choose 3–5 core topics that match your ICP's job-to-be-done searches
- [ ] Write pillar page first (broad, authoritative, links out to clusters)
- [ ] Write cluster pages over 4–8 weeks (don't publish all at once)
- [ ] Link every cluster back to the pillar AND to 2–3 other clusters
- [ ] Update pillar page monthly to reflect new clusters
- [ ] Monitor: organic impressions → clicks → trial signups by topic cluster

## Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)

CRO is about variance reduction, not individual wins. Teams running structured experiments generate 40× more revenue than teams that don't test.

**The CRO stack (by impact)**:
- Layer 1 — Copy & messaging: 60% of conversion variance
- Layer 2 — UX & design: 25% of conversion variance
- Layer 3 — Offer & pricing: 15% of conversion variance

Start with Layer 1. Changing the headline is almost always higher ROI than redesigning the page.

**Psychology levers that reliably move conversion** (use ethically):

| Lever | Mechanism | Implementation |
|---|---|---|
| Social proof | Conformity bias | "5,200 teams use this" · Star rating · Named logo bar |
| Anchoring | First number sets reference | Show annual price first → monthly feels cheap |
| Loss aversion | Losses hurt 2× more than gains | "Don't lose your data" > "Keep your data" |
| Scarcity | FOMO (use real scarcity only) | "3 spots left for onboarding this week" |
| Specificity | Specific numbers = credibility | "Saves 4.2 hours/week" > "Saves time" |
| Reciprocity | Give value first | Free audit / template before asking for email |

**Conversion lift benchmarks** from psychology-based CRO:
- Social proof + scarcity together: +15–30% conversion lift
- Paywall personalization (user's name): +17%
- Animated vs static paywall: 2.9× higher conversion
- Day-0 paywall (show right after value): highest; drops 50%+ after Day 7

**A/B test discipline**:
- Test one variable at a time (multivariate tests need 10× the traffic to reach significance)
- Minimum detectable effect: know what lift is worth measuring (< 5% lift usually isn't)
- Statistical significance: 95% minimum; 99% for pricing tests
- Run to completion: don't peek and stop early (p-hacking)

## Positioning: Do This Before Messaging

Positioning is strategic. Messaging is tactical. Build positioning first or messaging will drift.

**Positioning document structure** (5 elements):

1. **Who you serve** (ICP, not everyone): "Mid-market B2B SaaS teams of 50–200"
2. **Category you're in**: "Revenue operations platform" (don't invent new categories — anchor to existing mental models)
3. **"Why now"** (market shift that makes this timely): "AI cost just dropped 10× — manual sales ops is now 10× more expensive relative to automation"
4. **Value pillars** (3–5 business outcomes, each with proof): "Closes deals 30% faster · Reduces ramp time from 6 months to 8 weeks · 120% NRR from existing accounts"
5. **Competitive differentiation** (why you specifically): be honest about 1–2 things you do genuinely better than alternatives

**Impact**: Clear positioning increases brand recall by 40%; improves sales velocity 20–30%.

## Launch Playbook

**T-30 days**: Positioning locked; landing page live; waitlist or early-access open
**T-14 days**: Assets created (Figma MCP → hero image, social cards, email header)
**T-7 days**: Customer announcement email drafted; internal team briefed; sales enablement ready
**Launch day**: Customer email sent; Product Hunt / social posts live; Slack channel posted; baseline metrics captured
**T+7 days**: First cohort analysis (who engaged? who didn't?); non-adopter re-engagement email
**T+30 days**: Launch post-mortem: what channels drove signups? what messaging resonated?

**Key launch metrics to track**:
- Signup/trial rate by traffic source (which channel drove quality signups)
- Day-1 activation rate (did signups complete core action within 24 hours)
- Referral rate (did any signups come from word-of-mouth)
- NPS at Day 14 (early signal on retention)
